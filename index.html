How to play

Swap tiles until every full row and column contains the numbers 1 to 7, each exactly once.

  • Drag a tile onto another to swap them, or tap one and then another.
  • Tiles turn teal when they're in the right place. Teal tiles are locked.
  • Orange tags show the sum of the two tiles their arrows point to: one to the side and one above or below. A tag showing 10 that points to a 4 and one unknown tile means the unknown is 6.
  • You have 20 swaps. Every puzzle can be solved in 15, and each swap you have left earns a ★.
  • Swapped the wrong tiles? Press Undo within 10 seconds to reverse the swap and get it back. Shortcut: Ctrl+Z or U.
  • There's no daily limit, so play as many puzzles as you like.
